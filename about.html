About me

The woman
behind the frame

I see modelling as the art of becoming: stepping into a feeling, embodying a story or a garment, and finding new ways to be seen.

I’m Abigail Murugi, a Kenyan model drawn to fashion, beauty and visual storytelling. I’m fascinated by how a gaze can speak, how movement can transform an image, and how beauty can exist beyond convention.

My approach is rooted in curiosity, expression and a love of making. Whether it’s a beauty portrait, an editorial concept or the charge of a runway, I’m drawn to work that leaves something behind: a feeling, a thought, a moment that lingers.

As I grow in this craft, I’m looking to collaborate with creatives and brands who see beauty as expansive, individual and always evolving.
